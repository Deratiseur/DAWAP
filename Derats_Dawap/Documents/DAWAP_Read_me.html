Introduction

A pack of 59 kits designed to change the way you play.

Compatibility: compatible with all EE games and all other mods. Preferably (but not necessarily) install it after mods that add or modify spells (e.g. Spell Revision).

Click a kit's name to expand or collapse its description.

Mages

Scourge · Enraged · Spell stealer · Mangacian · Legendary singularity · Master combiner · Spheromaniac · Artillerist · Spell modeler · Unwise · Vampiric Arcanologist
Scourge

Advantage:

  • Restores all his spells and regains his innate abilities every 5 seconds.

Disadvantages:

  • Can only learn spells from the Evocation and Necromancy schools.
  • Cannot be of good alignment.
  • Cannot dual-class.

Notes:

  • This kit also installs two new level 1 Evocation spells (Fire Blast and Fiery Burst), needed to provide the minimum of 6 spells you must choose when creating a character in BG2 (7 in TOB).
  • Not available in IWDEE (magic schools are different).
  • Overpowered multi-class: Scourge/Cleric (all beneficial cleric spells + Heal every round).
Enraged

Advantages:

  • Whenever he kills a creature he gains Improved Alacrity for one round.
  • +15% damage dealt, weapon or spell.

Disadvantages:

  • He gives no credit to defensive schools and does not learn any spells from the schools of Abjuration, Divination, and Illusion.
  • When his hit points drop below 25%, he becomes a spell berserkerer. (For 3 rounds, the Enraged cannot move or attack. He can only think of casting spells: he can cast them without a pause, faster (-2 to casting time), but with a 10% chance of spellcasting failure.)

Notes:

  • Playing with schools is not a simple exercise: because of this, some spells are "green" and you MUST choose at least one of them when creating your character. However, there is no problem with memorizing spells afterwards.
  • Not available in IWDEE (magic schools are different).
Spell stealer

Advantages:

  • At will, by touch, can make an arcanist forget a spell (no saving throw) to restore one of his own spells of the highest level he knows.
  • Once per day, by touch, can make an arcanist forget all their spells (save vs. Death) to restore all his own spells.

Disadvantages:

  • Can memorize one fewer spell per spell level.
  • Access to each new spell level is delayed by one mage level (first level 1 spell at mage level 2).
Mangacian

Advantages:

  • He knows only one spell, Magic Missile, which he can cast at will and of which he is the specialist:
  • 1 additional missile every 3 levels, up to a maximum of 11 missiles.
  • Projectile damage scales with level:

Levels 1–6: 1D4+1

Levels 7–12: 1D6+2

Levels 13–18: 1D8+3

Levels 19–24: 1D10+4

Level 25 to 30: 1D12+5

Level 31 and up: 3D4+6

  • Each missile has a 5% chance of inflicting double damage, 10% at level 15, 15% at level 30.
  • Spell protection considers this spell to be of equal level to the number of missiles sent (IE: considered a level 6 spell when 6 missiles are sent). At 10 missiles sent, no spell protection can block this spell, apart from Shield and Protection against Evocation.

Disadvantages:

  • He can't learn any other spells.
  • He has no access to any HLAs.

Notes:

  • The Kit also installs a player script called "PXMANGA". Don't forget to use it:)
  • To use the Magic Missile, put it on a quick spell slot. Don't forget to assign a keyboard/mouse key to this quick spell shortcut.
  • When creating a character in BG2EE, you MUST choose spells. Choose anything you like anyway, as all spells learned are forgotten as soon as the game starts.
  • Find another way to give him "Improved Alacrity" and he becomes a god. (A very simple way: dual-class Dweomerist → Mangacian.)
Legendary singularity

Advantage:

  • Damage inflicted tripled.

Disadvantages:

  • Hit points fixed at 1 per level.
  • Not affected by effects that increase the number of maximum hit points.
  • Physical, elemental and magic resistances: -50%.
  • Saving throws: -4.

Notes:

  • Multi-class to try: Legendary Singularity/Fighter (a glass cannon that hits for 60-75 damage with a +3 weapon and Strength 19).
  • Multi-class to try: Legendary Singularity/Fighter Archer (few hit points, but he stays at the back).
  • Another multi-class to try: Thief/Legendary Singularity (a glass cannon that backstabs for 15D6).
  • Alternatively, dual-classing also works: Legendary Singularity at level X → fighter or thief (assassin for backstabs of 21D6).
Master combiner

Advantages:

  • He automatically learns combination spells as spells one level down.
  • At 5th level he gains the ability Spell Combination, which he can use once a day.

SPELL COMBINATION:

This spell allows you to combine spells and cast the result from the special abilities button. The number and level of spells that can be combined depend on the Master Combiner's level:

Level 5 to 8: 2 spells up to level 3

Level 9 to 12: 2 spells up to level 4

Level 13 to 16: 2 spells up to level 5

Level 17 to 20: 3 spells up to level 5

Level 21 to 24: 3 spells up to level 6

Level 25 to 28: 3 spells up to level 7

Level 29 and +: 3 spells up to level 8

Once the spell combination has been discharged, the button disappears.

  • All spell combinations (by a spell or its special ability) it triggers are enhanced: +35% damage and duration.

Disadvantage:

  • Transmuter and Invoker at the same time, but without additional spells.

Note:

  • Spell combination spells: Minor Sequencer (lvl 3), Spell Sequencer (lvl 6), Spell Trigger (lvl 7), Dillpater's Triple Dweomer (new spell, level 9)
  • Transmutator sucks because opposite Abjuration is too penalizing? In two words: Polymorph other (-2 to saving throw).
  • In IWDEE, this kit has 4 opposite schools. Ouch!
  • Multi-class to try: Master Combiner/Cleric (Minor Sequencer with Bless + Chant, casting time 1; Spell Sequencer with 3x level 15 Animate Dead, a level 3 cleric spell; Spell Trigger with 3x Aerial Servant; Dillpater’s Triple Dweomer with 3x Summon Deva—overpowered: normally limited to 1, but simultaneous casting bypasses the limit—or 3x Gate or 3x Elemental Summoning, all level 7 cleric spells).
Spheromaniac

Advantages:

  • For each spell level he knows two orb/sphere/globe spells that he can cast indefinitely:

Level 1 infinite spells: Chromatic Orb and Magic Missile

Level 2 infinite spells: Otiluke's Resilient Sphere and Cone of Elemental Orbs

Level 3 infinite spells: Explosive Orbs and Fireball

Level 4 infinite spells: Minor Globe of Invulnerability and Black Sphere

Level 5 infinite spells: Vitriolic Sphere and Otiluke's Freezing Sphere

Level 6 infinite spells: Lethal Sphere of Total Dissipation and Electric Sphere

Level 7 infinite spells: Lethal Sphere of Total Vulnerability and Delayed Blast Fireball

Level 8 infinite spells: Globe of Blades and Globe of Total Invulnerability

Level 9 infinite spells: Ice Orb and Globe of Absolute Immunity

  • All his spells are "on target", even normally personal spells (such as Globe of Invulnerability).

Disadvantages:

  • He can't learn any other spells or HLAs.
  • He cannot use scrolls.

Notes:

  • No "Antimagic Shell" spell because "on target" can make warriors and thieves immune to magic, that's OP.
  • When creating a character in BG2EE, you MUST choose spells. Choose anything you like anyway, as all spells learned are forgotten as soon as the game starts.
  • Find an alternative way to give it "Improved Alacrity" and it becomes a god (a very simple way: Dweomerist → Spheromaniac).
Artillerist

Advantages:

  • When he casts the "Melf's Minute Meteors" spell, he always creates 20 meteors, 30 at level 18, 40 at level 24 and 50 at lvl 30.
  • When he casts the "Energy Blades" spell, he always creates 30 blades, 40 at level 24 and 50 at level 30.
  • +1 to THAC0 with projectile weapons every 5 levels.
  • Can specialize in darts and slings.

Disadvantages:

  • He can't be Good.
  • The projectiles he creates last a maximum of one turn and disappear automatically if he moves.
Spell modeler

Advantages:

  • He models spells: whenever he should cast a spell, instead he can cast any existing spell of the corresponding level.
  • Casting time: -2.
  • He doesn't need to learn HLAs; he can cast them as level 9 spells from level 23.
  • Hit Die: D6.

Disadvantages:

  • Cannot "learn" spells.
  • Cannot cast spells "normally".
  • If he tries to cast Contingency/Sequencer spells, he's wasting his time, it won't work.
  • Each time he models a spell, he loses one hit point.

Notes:

  • When creating a character in BG2EE, you MUST choose spells. Choose anything you like anyway, as all spells learned are forgotten as soon as the game starts.
Unwise

Advantages:

  • +3 spells per spell level.
  • Maximum spell casting time set at 6.
  • Hit Die: D6.

Disadvantages:

  • Each time he kills a creature, all members of the group suffer 1D4 points of non-lethal damage.
  • Wisdom : -3.

Note:

  • If the Unwise kills several creatures at once (e.g. with a Fireball), the party takes the damage only once.
Vampiric Arcanologist

(changed animation: blue doppelganger)

Advantages:

  • He models spells: whenever he should cast a spell, instead he can cast any existing spell of the corresponding level.
  • He can also model clerical and druidic spells, and casts quest spells as level 8 spells.
  • +2 spells per spell level.

Disadvantages:

  • Cannot "learn" spells.
  • Cannot cast spells "normally".
  • He has no access to mage's HLAs and epic spells.
  • Each time he models a spell, all creatures within a 4.5-meter radius of him (including party members) lose hit points equal to half the level of the spell cast, rounded up.
  • Contingency/sequencer spells don't affect him.
  • Alignment limited to evil.

Anomaly

(changed animation: magic elemental)

Advantages:

  • Immune to offensive magic and 100% magic resistance.
  • Immune to poison, disease and level drain.
  • Immune to dead magic, miscast and imposed wild surges.
  • Gains two hit pointss each time he casts a spell.
  • Can cast a ball of energy +1 once per round at will, inflicting 1D4+1 magical damage. The "enchantment" level and damage increase by +1 per 5 levels of the anomaly, up to a maximum of +7.

Disadvantages:

Notes:

  • Its "Every time it casts a spell" advantage doesn't trigger with Improved Alacrity and doesn't work in combat if the Anomaly's combat script is the "super new script with checkboxes included in EE".

Spirit of the Mirror

(animation: constantly changing color)

Advantages:

  • Unlimited Spell Turning.
  • Light Reflection: +3 to AC.
  • Automatically learns all image spells when he reaches the required level.
  • Can cast image spells on a target.
  • Can cast personal versions of image spells as spells one level lower.

Disadvantage:

  • Very fragile: -50% hit points and -100% crushing damage resistance.

Note:

  • Image spells affected: Reflected Image, Mirror Image, Major Reflected Image (new spell, level 4), Mislead, Project Image and Simulacrum.
  • The mirror spirit receives two copies of each spell: the "personal" version at reduced level and the "on-target" version at normal level. Spell names are changed, but only by the descriptive text, as some mods can change the effect of spells (like Spell revision, for example).
  • When choosing your spells, avoid taking image spells, as this would be a waste.
  • Yes, this kit is overpowered: it can cast Mislead on a thief.
Sorcelerer

Advantages:

  • +1 proficiency point in daggers and quarterstaffs at level 1 and every 6 levels thereafter, up to Grand Mastery at level 24.
  • +½ attack per round at levels 9, 17 and 23.
  • +1 to melee THAC0 every 2 levels.
  • +1 to saves vs. Death every 4 levels.
  • +1 to saves vs. Polymorph every 8 levels.
  • Can advance in the Two-Handed Weapon, Single-Weapon and Two-Weapon fighting styles.
  • Knows improved versions of certain combat spells.
  • Can choose fighter HLAs in addition to mage HLAs.
  • Hit Die: D6.

Disadvantages:

  • Can cast two fewer spells per spell level.
  • Progression is slowed by the non-exclusive practice of magic: -15% XP.
  • Immune to effects that change XP received.
  • Cannot use ranged weapons.

IMPROVED SPELLS: the Sorcelerer spontaneously knows a few combat spells, which he improves when casting them:

  • True Strike: extended duration: 1 round/3 levels.
  • Armor: reduced casting time and improved AC: -1 AC/3 levels (best AC 0).
  • Strength: improved strength when casting upon himself: set at 19
  • Tenser's Deadly Strikes: always maximum duration and +5% critical strike chance.
  • Fire Shield (Red/Blue): affected weapon: +1D8 damage

Notes:

  • With the "Tweak Anthology" mod and its "Everyone Gets Bonus APR from Specialization" component, the Sorcelerer easily reaches 5 attacks per round.
  • With the "Semi-multi-cleric" mod, the Sorcelerer can gain priest spells (Draw Upon Holy Might, Righteous Magic, Blade Barrier, Heal, healing spells, Insect Plague, etc.) and become extremely powerful.
  • The Sorcelerer has problems with mods that change proficiencies (Skills and Abilities, Tweak Anthology, etc.): he loses his dagger and quarterstaff proficiencies and instead gains others that are useless to him. Use EE Keeper to fix this.
  • This mod cannot be installed on IWDEE: it is buggy on IWDEE and does not receive any XP.
Zap

Advantages:

  • +10 to movement speed.
  • +1 Attack per round.
  • -4 to casting time.
  • Improved Alacrity.
  • +3 to saving throws against wands and breaths.

Disadvantage:

  • When he does NOTHING he loses one hit point every 3 seconds.

Notes:

  • At least two rings of regeneration or similar items to cancel his disadvantage.
  • His advantages are NOT Haste and can therefore stack with Haste.
Psycho absorber

Advantages:

  • Every 3 rounds, he restores one spell of the highest level he knows. If there is no spell to restore at that level, he restores a spell from the next lower spell level instead.
  • Permanent Spell Trap.
  • Every time he kills a creature, he restores a spell of the highest level he knows.
  • Casting time fixed to 1.
  • After each rest he restores 1D10 spells of the maximum known level.

Disadvantage:

  • Any loss of consciousness: sleep (after a rest or induced), stun, fear, berserk, charm, confusion, feeblemindedness, or death causes him to forget all his spells.
Overflower

Advantages:

  • All his attacks add 1D4 points of magic damage. At higher levels, other random effects are added (all non-damaging effects allow a saving throw against spells to cancel and last one turn):

From level 4 onwards, all his attacks have an additional 5% chance of slowing down the target.

From level 8 all attacks have an additional 5% chance of stunning the target.

From level 12 onwards, all attacks have an additional 5% chance of adding 1D8 points of magic damage.

From level 16 onwards, all his attacks have an additional 5% chance of holding the target.

From level 20 onwards, all attacks have an additional 5% chance of disintegrating the target.

From level 24 all attacks have an additional 5% chance of adding 1D12 points of magic damage.

From level 28 onwards, all magical effects of his attacks are applied to a small area and affect only enemies.

  • Spells targeting him are disrupted: +15% resistance to magic.
  • Spells he casts are enhanced: +15% duration and elemental and magical damage inflicted, and +3 at caster level.

Disadvantage:

  • The spells he casts are disrupted: 15% chance of miscast.
Spellrage

Advantages:

  • Can become enraged 1 time per day, plus 1 use every 4 levels thereafter. The rage has the same effects as Tenser’s Transformation and berserker rage, and lasts 5 rounds.
  • From level 5, while enraged, gains +1 to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution per 5 levels (maximum +4).
  • From level 10, while enraged, gains an additional 1/2 attack per round per 5 levels (maximum +2 attacks per round).
  • From level 15, can cast spells while enraged, with a 10% chance of spellcasting failure.
  • From level 20, the rage lasts 1 turn.
  • From level 25, all damage dealt while enraged is increased by 33%.
  • From level 30, spellcasting can no longer fail while enraged, and gains +2 to casting speed.

Disadvantages:

  • Cannot use ranged weapons.
  • Becomes fatigued after raging, like a berserker.
  • -1 spell per spell level.

Necrophorus

Pyroclasm

(changed animation: fire elemental)

Advantages:

  • 125% fire resistance.
  • Immune to normal weapons and +1.
  • Immune to diseases, poisons, sneak attacks, webs, entangle and grease.
  • Knows about ten additional fire spells.
  • Inflicts 5% more fire damage per level, maximum +100%.
  • Can attack with its fire fist or with a small fireball whose damage and enchantment level vary according to the pyroclasm's level: 1D4+1 at level 1, 1D6+2 at level 5, 1D8+3 at level 10, 1D10+4 at level 15 and 1D12+5 at level 20.
  • Can be of any alignment.

Disadvantages:

  • Vulnerability to cold 50%.
  • Cannot carry any object.
  • Cannot metamorphose.
  • Does not gain other elemental resistances.

Notes:

  • Additional fire spells: Burning Hands, Agannazar Scorcher, Flame Arrow, Fireball, Melf Minute Meteors, Fire Shield (red) (lvl3), Sunfire (lvl 4), Delayed Blast Fireball (lvl 5), Summon Efreet (lvl5), Fire Storm (moved lvl 6), Incendiary Cloud (lvl 7), Dragon's Breath (HLAs)
  • His little fireball requires the dart skill.
Unstable Mutant Monstrosity

Advantage:

  • At level 1 and every 5 levels thereafter: +1 in strength, dexterity and constitution and -1 in intelligence, wisdom and charisma. (maximum +7/+7/+7/-7/-7/-7 at level 30).

Disadvantages:

  • He is charged with highly unstable energy and consequently once a round when he suffers damage he has a 5% chance of being disintegrated by generating a ball of magical energy which inflicts 10D10 points of magical damage to all creatures within 4.5 meters of him (saving throw against spells for ½).
  • When dying, he is automatically disintegrated and generates the ball of magical energy.

Note:

  • "Whenever he takes damage" means once per round in the event of poison, disease, insect plague or other such stuff. Be very vigilant.
Horde spirit

Advantage:

  • All his animal and elemental summoning spells (not archomental) have a doubled effect.

Disadvantage:

  • Knows no healing or regeneration spells.

Notes:

  • His advantage only affects spells from the unmodded game. It does not support spells added by mods. (Exception: it supports IWDEE’s "Giant Insects" and "Stalker" even in BG(2)EE.)
  • By default, only 5 summoned creatures can be in play, which limits the effects of doubled spells. I recommend removing the summoning limit with one of the many tweak mods that do this.
  • His advantage does not work in combat if the Horde Spirit uses the "super new script with checkboxes included in the EE games" as its combat script.
  • L’Interplan offers an amulet that adds a bear when Animal Summoning is used. A Horde Spirit wearing it can therefore summon 3 to 5 bears with each casting.
Ravager

Advantages:

  • Knows some twenty clerical and arcane elemental spells.
  • Casts some elemental spells at a lower level than he should.
  • Doubled elemental resistance.

Disadvantages:

  • Knows no creature summoning spells.
  • Has no interest in metamorphosis.

Saïyan Healer

Advantages:

  • He knows several spells of destruction: Magic Missile, Agannazar's Scorcher, Fireball, Vitriolic Sphere, Cone of Cold, Chain Lightning and Energy Blades.
  • His dance grants him +15% damage dealt and -1 to casting time for one turn. These bonuses stack. (For example, if he dances for 5 rounds, he will deal +60% damage and gain -4 to casting time for one turn when he stops dancing.)
  • At level 28, the bonuses granted by his dance are doubled, granting him Improved Alacrity and Improved Haste for 2 rounds and turning his hair golden.
  • His fist attacks evolve with his level as follows:

At level 1: 1D6 non-lethal damage, +1 to hit, 1.5 attacks per round, considered a +1 weapon.

At Level 4: 1D8 non-lethal damage, +2 to hit, 2 attacks per round

At level 8: 1D10 non-lethal damage, +3 to hit, 2.5 attacks per round, considered a +2 weapon

At level 12: 1D12 non-lethal damage, +4 to hit, 3 attacks per round

At level 16: 2D6 non-lethal damage, +5 to hit, 3.5 attacks per round, considered a +3 weapon

At level 20: 2D8 non-lethal damage, +6 to hit, 4 attacks per round

At level 24: 2D10 non-lethal damage, +7 to hit, 4.5 attacks per round, treated as a +4 weapon.

At level 28: 2D12 non-lethal damage, +8 to hit, 5 attacks per round.

Disadvantages:

  • He cannot use projectile weapons.
  • He cannot use one-handed weapons or shields.
  • He does not gain unique shamanic spells.

Notes:

  • Since the effects of his dance last only one turn, he does not receive the full bonuses for an entire turn. For example, if he dances for 6 rounds:

He dances for 1 round.

He dances for 2 rounds: +15% damage and -1 to casting time for one turn.

He dances for 3 rounds: +30% damage and -2 to casting time for 9 rounds (the 1st effect has already lasted one round).

He dances for 4 rounds: +45% damage and -3 to casting time for 8 rounds (the 1st effect has already lasted two rounds, and the 2nd one round).

He dances for 5 rounds: +60% damage and -4 to casting time for 7 rounds (the 1st effect has already lasted three rounds, the 2nd two rounds, and the 3rd one round).

He dances for 6 rounds: +75% damage and -5 to casting time for 6 rounds (etc.).

He stops dancing and can cast spells with -5 to casting time and +75% damage for 6 rounds, then 60%/-4 in the 7th round, 45%/-3 in the 8th round, 30%/-2 in the 9th round, and 15%/-1 in the 10th round.

  • Why do his fists deal non-lethal damage? Simple: in manga, heroes never kill their opponents; they just knock them out. (Personally, I think that is lame: the boss is merely knocked out and the story arc is over.)
  • Level 35 requires more than 8M XP and is therefore only available if you install an unlimited XP patch.
